# Literature audit

Reviewed through 27 November 2026.

- **Primary question and terminology.** Samuel Hand’s 2025 Glasgow thesis, §2.1.2, printed pp. 21–22, defines spread before ignition, ignitions at currently unburnt vertices and the final spread at timestep ℓ+1 (Definition 17). Definition 18, printed p. 35, uses the inclusive interval between each edge’s first and last appearance. The open question in §6.1, printed p. 94, asks about parameterization by that width and anticipates hardness for small width. Bumpus and Meeks introduced the parameter in Definition 2 of their edge exploration paper. [Hand thesis](https://doi.org/10.5525/gla.thesis.84865) · [Bumpus and Meeks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00453-022-01018-7)
- **Earlier complexity.** Hand’s Theorem 4 embeds static Graph Burning by making each edge appear throughout the relevant rounds. The thesis gives fixed-parameter tractability by vertex-interval-membership width and temporal neighbourhood diversity. Theorem 19 bounds the edge width for the distinct Temporal Firefighter problem at two. [Hand thesis](https://theses.gla.ac.uk/84865/3/2024HandPhD.pdf)
- **Closest published hardness.** Enright, Hand, Larios-Jones and Meeks establish NP-hardness at bounded temporal modular-width (MFCS 2024 Theorem 11, JCSS Theorem 5). Their leaf contacts are single-appearance. They schedule at least $2(h+1)$ simultaneous leaf contacts at each variable deadline and $3(h+1)$ at each clause deadline, with $h=2n+3m$. The edge-interval-membership width therefore grows. The journal/preprint strategy definitions start at timestep zero, allow ignitions on already burning vertices, and require completion at the final listed timestep. This candidate explicitly uses the thesis convention. [MFCS proceedings](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2024.52) · [JCSS issue volume 163](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/392700/) · [arXiv v2, 21 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.19453v2)
- **Adjacent work.** Enright et al.’s SAND 2026 paper and latest arXiv version study vertex and tree interval parameters and identify edge-interval membership as a future direction. Kutner and Larios-Jones study reachability from source sets on happy temporal graphs, with a different decision rule. Deligkas et al.’s earlier AAAI search hit is exact edge-cover by temporal journeys. [SAND 2026](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAND.2026.4) · [arXiv v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15699v5) · [Temporal reachability dominating sets](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06999v2) · [AAAI exact edge-cover record](https://cris.technion.ac.il/en/publications/how-many-lines-to-paint-the-city-exact-edge-cover-in-temporal-gra/)
- **Later survey.** Kitty Meeks’s June 2026 survey cites the MFCS work. The official abstract and references were checked. The survey’s full text was unavailable. [Cambridge University Press](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009766012.004)

Targeted searches crossed “temporal graph burning,” “edge-interval-membership width,” “one edge per timestep,” “single appearance,” and “Set Cover” with the authors and current follow-ups. No earlier classification at edge-interval-membership width one surfaced. Confidence is high in the statement match and in the comparison to the papers read, and moderate about priority within the checked literature.
