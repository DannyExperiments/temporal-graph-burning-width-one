# Claim scope and limitations

The theorem concerns Hand’s Temporal Graph Burning decision problem with spread before each ignition and a final spread after the last ignition. Edge-interval-membership width counts the maximum number of inclusive edge lifetimes covering a timestep. The hard instances use singleton lifetimes at distinct times and have a connected underlying graph containing an edge.

The graph lifetime in the construction can exceed the ignition budget. The reverse direction uses late leaves whose first contacts occur beyond the latest allowed final spread. Combined parameters, fixed graph classes beyond the stated restrictions, and other burning conventions remain outside the claim. The finite replay is corroborative. The proof is analytic.
