The legal strategy has 14 ignitions, with one more spread allowed at round 15. Late leaves T1 to T12 have no contact until round 16 or later.

Each edge’s interval is a singleton. The displayed contacts therefore give edge-interval-membership width one across the entire timeline, including the late leaves.
