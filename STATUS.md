# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/temporal-graph-burning-width-one/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24568155)

The result answers Hand’s edge-interval-membership-width question. With the thesis’s ignition and stopping rules, the problem is NP-complete at width one even for connected underlying graphs and singleton contacts at distinct times.

The proof has undergone internal mathematical review. External peer review and formalization are pending. The exact replay corroborates the proof on finite instances. The [literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) records relevant prior results and the limits of the search.

The manuscript, proof and verifier match the [reviewed commit](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/temporal-graph-burning-width-one/commit/c1ab99dda7c4d0e053331f73ec180e0b7564215b). The README links the repository, replay and local-source PDF build workflows.
